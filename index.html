Bulk Carrier Draft Surveys

Our Bulk Carrier Draft Surveys provide independent determinations of loaded and discharged cargo quantities, serving as the definitive record for your shipments. By meticulously measuring the vessel's draft marks and calculating changes in displacement before and after operations, we ensure that every metric ton is strictly accounted for.

Conducted by experienced maritime professionals, our surveys rigorously account for all critical variables—from local water density and consumable changes to complex hull deformations like hogging and sagging. Executed to the highest international standards, we deliver transparent, indisputable cargo weight certifications that safeguard your commercial interests and prevent costly discrepancies between shippers, charterers, and receivers.

Key Survey Services

Initial & Final Draft Surveys

Precise draft mark readings and displacement calculations before and after cargo operations, establishing the definitive record of quantity loaded or discharged.

Deadweight Calculations

Rigorous deadweight and constant determinations accounting for water density, consumables, and hull deflection — the foundation of an accurate, defensible cargo figure.

Ballast Water Monitoring

Careful sounding and verification of ballast tanks throughout operations, eliminating one of the most common sources of draft survey error and dispute.

Independent Cargo Verification

Neutral, third-party certification of cargo quantities that protects your commercial interests in disputes between shippers, charterers, and receivers.
